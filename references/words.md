# Word and phrase rules

Use these lists to prioritize edits, not to guess who wrote the text. Context decides.

- **Tier 1:** replace by default unless the term is required by a source, quotation, proper name, or technical field.
- **Tier 2:** check in clusters. One precise use may be fine.
- **Tier 3:** check at density. Keep precise uses and replace vague repetition with facts.

## Judgment rules

- Keep a required technical term and explain it once.
- Keep a precise use of a flagged word. Replace decorative or repeated uses.
- Repeat the correct noun when a synonym would make the subject harder to track.
- Do not swap words mechanically. Rewrite the clause when the sentence shape is the problem.
- Quoted examples, proper names, and source language are exempt.
