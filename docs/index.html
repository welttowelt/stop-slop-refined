Turn AI drafts into clear writing.

Stop Slop is a free editing skill for Claude, ChatGPT, and Codex. It removes generic wording and repeated sentence patterns while preserving your facts and voice.

01 / What it does

Fix more than
the buzzwords.

AI drafts can be grammatically clean and still sound generic. Stop Slop reviews the wording, sentence patterns, evidence, and voice before returning a clean rewrite.

  1. 01

    Generic wording

    Finds inflated vocabulary, canned openers, and filler that delays the point.

  2. 02

    Repeated templates

    Catches binary contrasts, forced triads, rhetorical setups, and uniform rhythm.

  3. 03

    Weak evidence

    Keeps uncertainty visible and stops a rewrite from inventing support.

  4. 04

    Voice drift

    Removes the AI texture without turning every writer into the same person.

Where it helps

Use it when a draft sounds polished but generic.

Work writing

Emails, memos, and leadership updates.

Public writing

Posts, website copy, and launch text.

Technical writing

Documentation, research notes, and product explanations.

Detect only

Review a draft and flag the problems without rewriting it.

How it works

The skill reviews the draft in this order.

  1. 01Protect factsClaim, evidence, uncertainty
  2. 02Check AI's roleWho chose the first shape?
  3. 03Fix structureArgument before polish
  4. 04Write plainlyCommon words, full logic
  5. 05Cut residueWords, patterns, formatting
  6. 06Read againAs a new reader
02 / Before line edits

Who chose the first shape?

If AI chose the claim, outline, and first example, word cleanup will preserve the model's center. Put the draft aside, recover the human point, then rebuild.

Read the AI-role check ↗
Anti-anchoring pass✓
  1. Put the current draft aside.
  2. Write the point without borrowing its nouns or order.
  3. List the evidence and uncertainty that must survive.
  4. Rebuild around the reader's needed action or understanding.

    04 / Core rules

    Eleven checks.
    One readable draft.

    The rules are ordered by reader consequence. They protect substance before they polish the sentence.

    1. 01

      Cut filler

      Remove throat-clearing, empty emphasis, decorative jargon, and hedges that do no useful work.

    2. 02

      Break formulas

      Rewrite binary contrasts, negative listings, rhetorical setups, and other repeated templates.

    3. 03

      Name responsibility

      Name the actor for judgment or choice. Keep ordinary literal subjects when they are clearer.

    4. 04

      Be specific

      Replace vague importance with the action, limit, reason, evidence, or consequence.

    5. 05

      Keep the reader close

      Use the actor, object, and situation instead of distant abstractions.

    6. 06

      Vary rhythm

      Mix sentence and paragraph shapes. Do not pad a list to three for cadence.

    7. 07

      Trust the reader

      State the point without reassurance or repeated justification.

    8. 08

      Cut manufactured quotables

      Rewrite lines built to sound profound instead of useful.

    9. 09

      Earn emphasis

      Show the change or tension instead of announcing it.

    10. 10

      Check the AI role

      Recover the human center before line editing an AI-first draft.

    11. 11

      Protect clarity

      Keep the logic, definition, or bridge that a first-time reader needs.
